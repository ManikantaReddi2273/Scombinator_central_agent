# Template: Welcome Onboard

## Use When
New employee has signed their offer letter and you're confirming start date and first-day details.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — New hire's first name
- `{{role_name}}` — Their position
- `{{start_date}}` — First day of work
- `{{start_time}}` — When to arrive/log in
- `{{manager_name}}` — Their manager's name
- `{{buddy_name}}` — Onboarding buddy (if assigned)
- `{{location}}` — Office location or "Remote"

---

## Subject Line
Welcome to Resolvely! 🎉 First day details

---

## Body

Hi {{name}},

We're excited to have you join as {{role_name}}!

**Your first day:** {{start_date}}
**Time:** {{start_time}} IST
**Location:** {{location}}

**What to expect on Day 1:**
- Laptop and accounts setup
- Meet the team
- 1:1 with {{manager_name}}
- Intro to how we work

**What to bring:**
- Government ID (for records)
- Any dietary preferences for lunch

**Before you start:**
- We'll send login credentials on {{start_date}} morning
- Join Slack when you get access — say hi in #general!

**Your onboarding buddy:** {{buddy_name}} will help you get settled. They'll reach out before your start date.

Questions before Day 1? Just reply to this email.

We're excited to have you!

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Notes
- Send within 24 hours of signed offer
- Be warm and specific about logistics
- Have buddy reach out BEFORE Day 1
- This sets the tone — make it great
