# Template: Rejection After Resume Review

## Use When
Candidate applied but didn't make it past resume review. No interview was conducted.

## Variables
- `{{candidate_name}}` — Candidate's first name
- `{{role_name}}` — Position they applied for

---

## Subject Line
Your application to Resolvely

---

## Body

Hi {{candidate_name}},

Thanks for taking the time to apply for the {{role_name}} position at Resolvely.

After reviewing applications, we've decided to move forward with candidates whose experience more closely matches what we're looking for right now.

This isn't a reflection of your abilities — these decisions often come down to very specific needs. We'd encourage you to apply again in the future as our needs evolve.

Thanks again, and best of luck with your search.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Notes
- Send within 48 hours of deciding not to interview
- Keep it brief — they didn't invest much time
- Never send on Fridays after 3 PM
