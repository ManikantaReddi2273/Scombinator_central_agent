# Template: Rejection After Interview

## Use When
Candidate completed interviews but didn't receive an offer. They invested time, so be more personal.

## Variables
- `{{candidate_name}}` — Candidate's first name
- `{{role_name}}` — Position they applied for
- `{{positive_note}}` — Something genuine you liked about them
- `{{reason}}` — Brief, honest reason (constructive, not harsh)

---

## Subject Line
Update on {{role_name}} role at Resolvely

---

## Body

Hi {{candidate_name}},

Thank you for taking the time to interview with us for the {{role_name}} role. I enjoyed our conversations, especially {{positive_note}}.

After careful consideration, we've decided to move forward with another candidate. {{reason}}

This was a close decision, and I want to be clear — this doesn't reflect a lack of talent on your part. Hiring often comes down to very specific needs and timing.

I'd genuinely encourage you to keep an eye on our openings as we grow. I could see you being a great fit for a future role.

If you'd like any introductions in your search, happy to help where I can.

Best of luck, and thanks again for your time.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Example

> Hi Amit,
>
> Thank you for taking the time to interview with us for the Full-Stack Engineer role. I enjoyed our conversations, especially your thoughtful approach to the system design problem.
>
> After careful consideration, we've decided to move forward with another candidate. It came down to specific experience with e-commerce integrations — something we need heavily in this particular hire.
>
> This was a close decision, and I want to be clear — this doesn't reflect a lack of talent on your part. Hiring often comes down to very specific needs and timing.
>
> I'd genuinely encourage you to keep an eye on our openings as we grow. Your problem-solving approach stuck with me.
>
> If you'd like any introductions in your search, happy to help where I can.
>
> Best of luck, and thanks again for your time.
>
> Best,
> Divya

---

## Notes
- Send within 24 hours of the decision
- NEVER send on Friday afternoon
- Include something positive and specific
- Give a real reason, but keep it constructive
- Phone call is better for final-round candidates
