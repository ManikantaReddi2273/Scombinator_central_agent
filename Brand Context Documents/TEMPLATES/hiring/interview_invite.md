# Template: Interview Invitation

## Use When
Inviting a candidate to interview after resume review shows they're a potential fit.

## Variables
- `{{candidate_name}}` — Candidate's first name
- `{{role_name}}` — Position they applied for
- `{{round_name}}` — Type of interview (Phone Screen, Technical, etc.)
- `{{duration}}` — Length of interview
- `{{interviewer_name}}` — Who they'll be meeting
- `{{interviewer_role}}` — Interviewer's title
- `{{scheduling_link}}` — Calendly or scheduling link

---

## Subject Line
Next step: {{role_name}} at Resolvely

---

## Body

Hi {{candidate_name}},

Thanks for applying for the {{role_name}} role — we'd like to move forward!

**Next step:** {{round_name}} ({{duration}}) with {{interviewer_name}}, our {{interviewer_role}}.

Pick a time that works: {{scheduling_link}}

A few notes:
- The call will be on Google Meet
- Come with questions — this is a two-way conversation

If none of those slots work, reply with your availability and we'll sort it out.

Looking forward to it!

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Variations

**For Technical Round:**
> **Next step:** Technical Interview (60 min) with {{interviewer_name}}, our Engineering Lead.
>
> What to expect:
> - A coding problem (you can use your preferred language)
> - Discussion about past projects
> - Time for your questions
>
> No trick questions — we want to see how you think.

**For Final Round:**
> **Next step:** Final Conversation (45 min) with {{interviewer_name}}, our [Founder/CTO].
>
> This is about values, long-term goals, and making sure we're a mutual fit. Come with questions.

---

## Notes
- Send within 24 hours of deciding to interview
- Always include what to expect
- Make scheduling easy — use a link
- Keep tone warm — good candidates have options
