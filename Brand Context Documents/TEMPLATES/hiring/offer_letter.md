# Template: Offer Letter Email

## Use When
Extending a job offer after completed interviews. 

**Important:** Always give a verbal offer (phone call) BEFORE sending this email.

## Variables
- `{{candidate_name}}` — Candidate's first name
- `{{role_name}}` — Position title
- `{{compensation}}` — Annual CTC
- `{{start_date}}` — Proposed joining date
- `{{response_deadline}}` — When you need a decision (5 business days)
- `{{reporting_to}}` — Who they'll report to
- `{{personal_note}}` — Something specific from the interview process

---

## Subject Line
Offer: {{role_name}} at Resolvely 🎉

---

## Body

Hi {{candidate_name}},

Great news — we'd love to have you join Resolvely as {{role_name}}!

As we discussed on the call:

**Role:** {{role_name}}
**Compensation:** {{compensation}} per annum
**Reporting to:** {{reporting_to}}
**Start Date:** {{start_date}}

**What's included:**
- Health insurance for you + family
- Stock options (ESOPs)
- Learning budget
- Flexible work hours

{{personal_note}}

**Next steps:**
1. Let us know your decision by {{response_deadline}}
2. If yes, we'll send the formal offer letter to sign
3. We'll kick off onboarding

Questions? Happy to hop on a call.

We're excited about the possibility of working together!

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Notes
- ALWAYS call before emailing
- Include a personal note — shows you remember them
- Be clear about deadline but not aggressive
- Make yourself available for questions
