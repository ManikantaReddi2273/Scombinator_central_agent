# Template: New Inquiry Reply

## Use When
Someone reaches out expressing interest in Resolvely. First response to establish contact.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{what_they_asked}}` — Brief reference to their inquiry
- `{{qualifying_question}}` — Question to understand their needs

---

## Subject Line
Re: [Their original subject]

---

## Body

Hi {{name}},

Thanks for reaching out! Happy to help with {{what_they_asked}}.

Quick question to point you in the right direction: {{qualifying_question}}

Happy to jump on a call if easier — let me know what works.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Examples

**Example 1: Vague inquiry**
> Hi Meera,
>
> Thanks for reaching out! Happy to tell you more about Resolvely.
>
> Quick question: What's driving you to look at support automation right now? High ticket volume, team bandwidth, something else?
>
> Happy to jump on a call if easier — let me know what works.
>
> Best,
> Arjun

**Example 2: Specific inquiry about how it works**
> Hi Rahul,
>
> Thanks for reaching out! Happy to explain how we work.
>
> Quick question: What helpdesk and e-commerce platform are you using? That'll help me explain how we'd integrate.
>
> Or if easier, we could do a quick 15-min call this week?
>
> Best,
> Arjun

---

## Notes
- Respond within 4 hours
- Ask ONE qualifying question — don't interrogate
- Always offer a call as an alternative
- Keep it short
