# Template: Demo Confirmation

## Use When
Confirming a scheduled product demo. Send after they book or agree on a time.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{date}}` — Demo date
- `{{time}}` — Demo time with timezone
- `{{meet_link}}` — Google Meet link

---

## Subject Line
Confirmed: Resolvely demo on {{date}}

---

## Body

Hi {{name}},

We're set for {{date}} at {{time}} IST.

**Meeting link:** {{meet_link}}

The demo is ~30 minutes. I'll show you how Resolvely works and dig into your specific setup.

To make this useful:
- What helpdesk are you using?
- What e-commerce platform?
- Roughly how many tickets/month?

No pressure to answer now — but it helps me tailor the demo.

See you {{date}}!

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Notes
- Send immediately after booking
- Include the meeting link clearly
- Ask prep questions but make them optional
- Keep tone warm and professional
