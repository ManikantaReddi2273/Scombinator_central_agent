# Template: Not a Fit Response

## Use When
After qualifying, you determine they're not a good fit for Resolvely. Be honest and helpful.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{reason}}` — Honest, brief reason why it's not a fit
- `{{alternative}}` — Suggestion for what might work for them

---

## Subject Line
Re: [Their original thread]

---

## Body

Hi {{name}},

Thanks for the context!

Honest take: I don't think Resolvely is the right fit for you right now. {{reason}}

{{alternative}}

If things change down the line, happy to revisit.

Appreciate you considering us!

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Examples

**Example 1: Too low volume**
> Hi Meera,
>
> Thanks for the context!
>
> Honest take: I don't think Resolvely is the right fit for you right now. At 150 tickets/month, the ROI on automation doesn't really work — you'd be paying for capacity you don't need.
>
> At your volume, a good helpdesk with canned responses (Freshdesk has a solid free tier) might be all you need.
>
> If volume grows to 500+, happy to revisit.
>
> Appreciate you considering us!
>
> Best,
> Arjun

**Example 2: B2B company**
> Hi Rahul,
>
> Thanks for reaching out!
>
> Honest take: Resolvely is built for B2C e-commerce — order tracking, returns, that kind of stuff. For B2B SaaS support, we wouldn't be the best fit.
>
> You might want to look at Intercom or Zendesk's AI features — they're more suited for B2B.
>
> If you ever launch a consumer product, let me know!
>
> Best,
> Arjun

---

## Notes
- Be honest but kind
- Always offer an alternative or suggestion
- Leave the door open for the future
- This builds goodwill — sometimes leads to referrals
