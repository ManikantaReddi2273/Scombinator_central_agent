# Template: Pricing Inquiry Response

## Use When
Someone asks about pricing via email. Route to a call instead of answering over email.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{scheduling_link}}` — Calendly or scheduling link (optional)

---

## Subject Line
Re: [Their original subject]

---

## Body

Hi {{name}},

Great question! Pricing depends on your setup — ticket volume, integrations, how you want to use it.

Rather than give you a generic number, I'd prefer to understand your situation and give you accurate pricing. It's a quick 15-minute call.

What works this week?

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Variations

**If they push for "just a ballpark":**
> I hear you — I could give a range, but it wouldn't be meaningful without understanding your volume and needs.
>
> It's genuinely a 15-minute call. I'll ask a few questions, then give you real numbers. What works?

**If they send their details proactively:**
> Thanks for the context! Based on what you've shared, I have a few follow-up questions to give you accurate pricing.
>
> Can we do a quick call? 15 minutes and I'll have numbers for you.

---

## Notes
- NEVER share specific pricing over email
- Always route to a call
- Keep it friendly, not evasive
- Make the call sound quick and valuable (not a sales pitch)
