# Template: Warm Intro Request

## Use When
Asking someone in your network to introduce you to someone they know.

## Variables
- `{{connector_name}}` — Person you're asking for the intro
- `{{target_name}}` — Person you want to meet
- `{{target_company}}` — Their company
- `{{reason}}` — Why you want to connect
- `{{forwardable_blurb}}` — 2-3 sentences they can forward

---

## Body

Hey {{connector_name}},

Quick favor — I'd love an intro to {{target_name}} at {{target_company}}.

{{reason}}

Totally fine if it's not the right time or you'd rather not — no pressure at all.

Here's a forwardable blurb if helpful:

---
{{forwardable_blurb}}
---

Thanks!

---

## Example

> Hey Sneha,
>
> Quick favor — I'd love an intro to Meera at Bewakoof.
>
> They're scaling fast and probably feeling support pain — exactly what we're solving. Would love to learn about their setup and see if there's a fit.
>
> Totally fine if it's not the right time or you'd rather not — no pressure at all.
>
> Here's a forwardable blurb if helpful:
>
> ---
> Hey Meera, wanted to connect you with Arjun. He's building AI support automation specifically for e-commerce — helping brands handle the "where's my order" type tickets automatically. Given Bewakoof's growth, thought it might be relevant. No pressure, but figured worth connecting in case it's useful.
> ---
>
> Thanks!

---

## Notes
- Keep the ask clear and easy to say no to
- Always provide a forwardable blurb — makes it easy for them
- Don't be pushy — if they don't respond, let it go
- Thank them regardless of outcome
