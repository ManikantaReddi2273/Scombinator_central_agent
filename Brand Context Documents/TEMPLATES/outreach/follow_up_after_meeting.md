# Template: Follow-Up After Meeting

## Use When
After an in-person or video meeting where you discussed their situation. This is the relationship-building meeting, NOT the product demo.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{topic_discussed}}` — Main thing you talked about
- `{{something_personal}}` — Personal detail from the conversation
- `{{next_step}}` — What you agreed on (if anything)
- `{{resource}}` — Something useful you can share (optional)

---

## Subject Line
Great catching up

*Or reply to existing thread*

---

## Body

Hey {{name}},

Really enjoyed our chat — {{something_personal}}.

{{topic_discussed}} — appreciate you sharing what's actually going on. That context is super helpful.

{{next_step}}

{{resource}}

Talk soon!

---

## Examples

**Example 1: They showed interest in a demo**
> Hey Priya,
>
> Really enjoyed our chat — the story about the Diwali sale support meltdown was both hilarious and terrifying.
>
> What you described with return requests eating 40% of your tickets — that's exactly the pain we're focused on. Thanks for being so open about what's working and not.
>
> Would love to show you what we've built when you're ready. Let me know when makes sense for a proper demo.
>
> Talk soon!
>
> Arjun

**Example 2: Good conversation, no clear next step yet**
> Hey Rahul,
>
> Really enjoyed our chat — glad to hear the new warehouse is finally operational!
>
> The support scaling challenges you mentioned are interesting. Definitely see how automation could help, but sounds like you've got a lot on your plate right now.
>
> No rush on anything — happy to pick this up whenever timing makes sense. In the meantime, here's that case study I mentioned: [link]
>
> Good luck with the launch!
>
> Arjun

**Example 3: They need to involve someone else**
> Hey Meera,
>
> Great catching up — always fun to hear what's happening at Bewakoof.
>
> Makes sense that Raj handles the support ops decisions. Happy to meet him whenever works — just let me know and I'll make time.
>
> Thanks again for the coffee!
>
> Arjun

---

## Notes
- Send within 24 hours of the meeting
- Reference something personal — shows you were actually listening
- Don't push if there wasn't clear interest
- If they want a demo, let them ask or suggest it naturally
- Keep it casual — this isn't a formal follow-up
