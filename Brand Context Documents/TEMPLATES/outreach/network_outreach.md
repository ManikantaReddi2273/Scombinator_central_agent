# Template: Network Outreach

## Use When
Reaching out to someone in your 1st degree network — people you know personally or have worked with before.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{personal_connection}}` — How you know them / shared history
- `{{their_recent_thing}}` — Something recent about them (role, company, achievement)
- `{{your_ask}}` — What you want (coffee, call, advice)

---

## Subject Line

For email (if not WhatsApp/LinkedIn):
- Quick catch up?
- Coffee soon?
- [No subject — just send if it's casual]

---

## Body

Hey {{name}},

{{personal_connection}}

{{their_recent_thing}} — [genuine comment about it].

We're building something in the e-commerce support space. Would love to get your take — [not a pitch / just want to learn from someone dealing with this / value your perspective].

{{your_ask}}?

[Casual sign-off]

---

## Examples

**Example 1: Former colleague**
> Hey Priya,
>
> Been a while since WebEngage! Hope you're doing well.
>
> Saw you're killing it at Purple — congrats on the expansion.
>
> We're building AI support automation for e-commerce. Would love to pick your brain — not a sales thing, just want to learn from someone who actually runs support at scale.
>
> Free for coffee next week? I'm around Indiranagar most days.
>
> Cheers,
> Arjun

**Example 2: Conference/event connection**
> Hey Rahul,
>
> We met at the D2C Founders meetup last month — you were talking about scaling NoiseFit's support.
>
> That stuck with me because we're building something in exactly that space. Would love to hear more about what you're seeing.
>
> Open to a quick call this week?
>
> Arjun

**Example 3: Mutual friend's introduction came through**
> Hey Meera,
>
> Thanks for connecting — Sneha speaks highly of you!
>
> She mentioned you're scaling support at Bewakoof. We're building AI automation for exactly this — would love to learn what's working and not working for you.
>
> Coffee sometime? Happy to come to your side of town.
>
> Arjun

---

## Notes
- Keep it SHORT — under 100 words
- Lead with the personal connection, not what you want
- Make the ask low-commitment (coffee, quick call)
- This is NOT a pitch — don't explain your product in detail
- Sound like yourself, not like a sales email
- WhatsApp or LinkedIn DM often works better than email for network contacts
