# Template: Soft Follow-Up

## Use When
Following up when you haven't heard back. Keep it light and low-pressure.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{context}}` — Brief reminder of what you discussed/proposed

---

## Subject Line
[Reply to existing thread — no new subject]

---

## Body

**Version 1: Casual bump**
> Hey {{name}}, just floating this up — any thoughts?

**Version 2: With context**
> Hey {{name}},
>
> Wanted to check in on {{context}}. No rush — just want to make sure this didn't get buried.
>
> Let me know what works.

**Version 3: Offering an out**
> Hey {{name}},
>
> Following up on {{context}}. If timing isn't right, totally fine — just let me know and I'll check back in a few months.

---

## Examples

**Example 1: After proposing a meeting**
> Hey Priya, just floating this up — still keen to grab coffee when you have time. This week or next?

**Example 2: After demo, waiting on their decision**
> Hey Rahul,
>
> Wanted to check in on next steps after our demo. No rush — just want to make sure you have what you need to decide.
>
> Any questions I can answer?

**Example 3: Long silence**
> Hey Meera,
>
> Following up on the pricing conversation. If timing isn't right, totally fine — just let me know and I'll check back in a couple months.

---

## Notes
- Keep it SHORT — 1-3 sentences max
- Don't guilt trip or be passive aggressive
- It's okay to offer them an out — sometimes that unsticks things
- Don't follow up more than 2-3 times total
- Space follow-ups at least 5-7 days apart
