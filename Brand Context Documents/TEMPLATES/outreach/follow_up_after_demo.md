# Template: Follow-Up After Demo

## Use When
After showing the product in a demo. They've seen what we do and expressed interest.

## Variables
- `{{name}}` — Their first name
- `{{their_use_case}}` — Specific thing they wanted to solve
- `{{key_feature}}` — Feature they were most interested in
- `{{relevant_resource}}` — Case study or material relevant to them
- `{{next_step}}` — Proposed next step (pricing call, trial, etc.)

---

## Subject Line
Re: [Previous thread]

*Or:* Following up on the demo

---

## Body

Hey {{name}},

Thanks for taking the time today — good to dig into {{their_use_case}}.

Quick recap of what we covered:
- {{key_feature}} — how that would work for your setup
- [Any other key points discussed]

As promised: {{relevant_resource}}

**Next step:** {{next_step}}

Let me know what questions come up.

Best,
[Name]

---

## Example

> Hey Priya,
>
> Thanks for taking the time today — good to dig into the return request automation.
>
> Quick recap of what we covered:
> - Auto-checking return eligibility against your policy
> - How it would integrate with Freshdesk
> - The 5-day setup timeline
>
> As promised, here's the Bewakoof case study — similar volume, similar pain: [link]
>
> **Next step:** Let's do a quick call to go through pricing specifics. I want to give you real numbers based on your volume. How's Thursday afternoon?
>
> Let me know what questions come up.
>
> Best,
> Arjun

---

## Notes
- Send within 2 hours of demo (while it's fresh)
- Keep recap to 3 bullets max
- Always include one relevant resource
- Pricing next step should be a CALL, not email (see POLICIES.md)
- Be specific about next step — don't be vague
