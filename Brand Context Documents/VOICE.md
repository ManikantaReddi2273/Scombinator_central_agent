# Brand Voice Guide

## Our Personality

**If our brand were a person, they would be:**
A sharp, friendly colleague who's worked in e-commerce support — they get your pain, skip the fluff, and give you straight answers.

**Three words that describe us:**
1. Practical
2. Friendly
3. Direct

**Three words that are NOT us:**
1. Corporate
2. Salesy
3. Over-promising

## Tone Adjustments by Context

| Situation | Adjust Tone |
|-----------|-------------|
| Network outreach | Personal, warm — like messaging a friend |
| Customer complaint | More empathetic, acknowledge frustration first |
| Technical explanation | Patient, use examples, avoid jargon |
| Job candidate | Warm, excited about the role, transparent |
| Rejection (candidate) | Kind but clear, no false hope |
| Not a fit (sales) | Gracious, helpful, leave door open |

## Writing Rules

### Always Do

- [x] **Get to the point.** First sentence should tell them why you're writing
- [x] **Use "you" and "your"** — make it about them, not us
- [x] **Use numbers when possible.** "70% of tickets" not "most tickets"
- [x] **Use contractions.** "We're" not "We are", "you'll" not "you will"
- [x] **One idea per paragraph.** Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- [x] **End with a clear next step.** What should they do now?

### Never Do

- [x] **Never open with "I hope this email finds you well"** — it's filler
- [x] **Never use "please do not hesitate to contact"** — just say "reply to this email"
- [x] **Never use "synergy", "leverage", "circle back", "touch base"**
- [x] **Never write more than 5 paragraphs** in any outreach
- [x] **Never use "I" as the first word** — start with them, not us
- [x] **Never make claims without backing them up.** "We're the best" = bad. "We resolved 45% more tickets for Bewakoof" = good

## Vocabulary Guide

### Words We Use

| Instead of... | We say... |
|---------------|-----------|
| Utilize | Use |
| Leverage | Use |
| Facilitate | Help |
| Implement | Set up |
| Commence | Start |
| Prior to | Before |
| In order to | To |
| At this point in time | Now |
| Reach out | Message / Email / Call |

### Our Phrases

**Phrases we like:**
- "Here's the thing..."
- "Quick question:"
- "The short version:"
- "Here's what that means for you:"
- "Happy to walk you through it"
- "Let me know what works"

**Phrases we avoid:**
- "Per my last email"
- "Just following up" (say WHY you're following up)
- "Hope this helps!" (too generic)
- "Let me know if you have any questions" (give them a specific CTA instead)
- "Please advise"
- "Kindly do the needful"

## Email Formatting

### Greetings

| Situation | Use This |
|-----------|----------|
| Network contact (personal) | Hey {{First Name}}, |
| Professional first contact | Hi {{First Name}}, |
| Formal (investor, legal) | Hello {{First Name}}, |
| Unknown name | Hi there, |
| Group email | Hi team, / Hi all, |

**Never use:**
- Dear Sir/Madam
- To whom it may concern
- Respected {{Name}}

### Sign-offs

| Situation | Use This |
|-----------|----------|
| Standard business | Best, {{Your Name}} |
| Friendly/personal | Cheers, {{Your Name}} |
| After someone helps you | Thanks, {{Your Name}} |
| Formal | Regards, {{Your Name}} |

**Never use:**
- Warm regards
- Kind regards
- Thanks and regards
- Best regards

### Subject Lines

| Rule | Guideline |
|------|-----------|
| Length | Under 50 characters |
| Case | Sentence case |
| Emojis | No |
| Clickbait | No — be clear about what's inside |

**Good subject lines:**
- Quick question
- Coffee next week?
- Following up — [topic]
- [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out

**Bad subject lines:**
- You won't believe this!
- URGENT: Please read
- Following up (with no context)
- RE: (fake reply)

### Email Body Structure

```
[Greeting]

[1-2 sentences: Context / why you're writing]

[2-3 sentences: The actual content/value]

[1 sentence: Clear next step]

[Sign-off]
```

**Formatting rules:**
- Paragraphs: 3 sentences max
- Total length: Under 100 words for network outreach
- Bold: Only for ONE key thing if needed
- Bullet points: Only if listing 3+ items
- Links: Maximum 1 per email (for outreach)

## Examples: Good vs Bad

### Network Outreach

**❌ Bad:**
> Dear Priya,
> 
> I hope this email finds you well. I am reaching out to introduce Resolvely, an AI-powered customer support platform. We leverage cutting-edge machine learning to help e-commerce brands reduce their ticket volume.
> 
> I would love the opportunity to schedule a call to discuss how we can help Purple achieve its customer support goals. Please let me know if you would be amenable to a brief conversation.
> 
> Best regards

**✅ Good:**
> Hey Priya,
>
> Been a while since WebEngage! Saw you're crushing it at Purple — congrats on the expansion.
>
> We're building something in the support automation space. Would love to get your take on it over coffee — not a pitch, just want to learn from someone who actually deals with this at scale.
>
> Free next week?
>
> Cheers,
> Arjun

---

### Customer Complaint Response

**❌ Bad:**
> Dear Customer,
> 
> Thank you for reaching out to Resolvely support. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused. Your feedback is valuable to us and we are committed to providing excellent service. Our team is looking into your issue and will revert shortly.
> 
> Best regards

**✅ Good:**
> Hi Meera,
>
> That's frustrating — I'd be annoyed too if responses were going out with wrong tracking info. Totally on us.
>
> Here's what happened: Our Shiprocket sync had a 2-hour lag yesterday. We've fixed it, and I've manually corrected the 12 affected tickets.
>
> I'm keeping an eye on this for the next 24 hours. If you see anything off, message me directly.
>
> Sorry again for the hassle.
>
> Priya

---

### Candidate Rejection

**❌ Bad:**
> Dear Applicant,
> 
> Thank you for your interest in Resolvely. After careful consideration, we regret to inform you that we have decided to move forward with other candidates whose qualifications more closely match our requirements.
> 
> We wish you all the best in your future endeavors.
> 
> Regards,
> HR Team

**✅ Good:**
> Hi Amit,
>
> Thanks for taking the time to interview with us — I enjoyed our conversation about support automation.
>
> After discussing with the team, we've decided to go with another candidate who has more hands-on e-commerce integration experience. It was a close call, and honestly came down to very specific needs for this particular role.
>
> I'd encourage you to apply again as we grow — your product thinking was sharp.
>
> Best of luck with the search.
>
> Divya

## Voice Checklist

Before sending, ask yourself:

- [ ] Does the first sentence explain why I'm writing?
- [ ] Is it short enough? (Under 100 words for outreach)
- [ ] Did I avoid all banned phrases?
- [ ] Is there ONE clear next step?
- [ ] Would I want to read this if I received it?
- [ ] Does it sound like a human wrote it?
- [ ] If it's to someone I know — does it sound like ME?
