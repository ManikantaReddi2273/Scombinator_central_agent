# Pricing & Commercial Policies

## Core Rule: Pricing = Direct Conversation

**We do not discuss specific pricing over email or in documents.**

When pricing comes up — in any form — route to a short call or meeting.

---

## When Pricing Comes Up

### If someone asks about pricing in initial outreach:

**Response:**
> "Pricing depends on your specific setup and volume — I want to make sure I give you accurate numbers rather than a generic range. Can we do a quick 15-min call to go through it?"

**Action:** Schedule a short call focused on understanding their needs and discussing pricing.

### If pricing comes up during a demo:

**Response:**
> "Great question — let's set up a quick follow-up call to go through pricing specifics. I want to understand your volume and needs better so I can give you the right numbers."

**Action:** Don't rush. Book a separate pricing call.

### If someone pushes for "just a ballpark":

**Response:**
> "I hear you — I'd rather give you accurate info than a number that doesn't reflect your situation. It's a 15-min call. What works this week?"

---

## Pricing Calls: What To Cover

In a pricing discussion, understand:
1. Their monthly ticket volume
2. Current support setup and costs
3. What success looks like for them
4. Timeline and decision process

Then discuss:
- Which plan fits their needs
- Any volume-based considerations
- Payment and contract terms
- Trial or pilot options

## Refund Policy

| Situation | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| Within 7 days, minimal usage | Discuss directly — likely full refund |

# Hiring Policies

## Approval Requirements

| Decision | Approver |
|----------|----------|
| Post new job opening | Leadership |
| Move candidate to interview | Hiring Manager |
| Reject candidate (after resume) | Hiring Manager or People |
| Reject candidate (after interview) | Hiring Manager |
| Make offer (within band) | Hiring Manager |
| Make offer (above band) | Hiring Manager + Leadership |
| Equity/ESOP grants | Leadership |

---

## Auto-Reject Criteria

**Do not interview if:**
- [ ] No legal right to work in India
- [ ] Expecting salary 30%+ above our band (and won't flex)
- [ ] Notice period > 60 days (and not negotiable)
- [ ] Role requires location presence, candidate won't relocate
- [ ] For senior roles: Less than 3 years relevant experience
- [ ] Candidate rejected in last 6 months (unless specifically invited)

---

## Offer Rules

- Offers valid for 5 business days
- No signing bonus without leadership approval
- No guaranteed bonus without leadership approval
- Start date must be within 60 days of offer acceptance
- Background verification required before joining

---

# Data & Privacy Policies

- [ ] Fundraising details beyond what's public

- [ ] Product roadmap specifics

---

## Safe to Share

- [x] Published case studies (with customer permission)
- [x] General company info (what we do, where we're based)
