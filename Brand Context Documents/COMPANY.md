## What We Do

Resolvely is an AI customer support platform built specifically for e-commerce and D2C brands. We handle repetitive customer queries — order status, returns, refunds, product questions — automatically, so support teams can focus on complex issues that actually need humans.

Our AI learns from each brand's past tickets, product catalog, and policies to give accurate, on-brand responses. We integrate with Shopify, WooCommerce, Gorgias, Freshdesk, and Zendesk.

---

## Why We Exist

**Problem:** E-commerce support teams drown in repetitive tickets. 70% of queries are "Where is my order?" or "How do I return this?" — answerable in seconds if you have the right data.

**Solution:** Resolvely's AI reads order data, shipping info, and return policies in real-time to auto-resolve these queries. Human agents only see tickets that actually need them.

**Mission:** Free support teams from robotic work so they can be genuinely helpful.

---

## Who We Serve

**Primary Customers:**
- D2C e-commerce brands doing ₹1-50 Cr annual revenue
- Brands with 500+ support tickets per month
- Companies using Shopify, WooCommerce, or custom platforms

**We Are NOT For:**
- B2B companies (our AI is trained for consumer queries)
- Brands with under 200 tickets/month (ROI won't make sense)
- Companies without existing helpdesk software
