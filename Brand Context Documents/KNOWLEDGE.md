# Company FAQ

**Q: What does Resolvely do?**
> Resolvely is an AI customer support platform built for e-commerce brands. We automatically handle repetitive queries like "Where's my order?" and "How do I return this?" so your support team can focus on complex issues. We integrate with your existing helpdesk (Freshdesk, Zendesk, Gorgias) and e-commerce platform (Shopify, WooCommerce).

**Q: How is this different from chatbots?**
> Most chatbots are rule-based — they follow scripts. Resolvely actually reads your order data, shipping info, and return policies in real-time. So when a customer asks "Where's my order?", we pull their actual tracking info and respond with specifics — not a generic "Please check your email for tracking info."

**Q: How long have you been around?**
> We started Resolvely in 2023. We work with 30+ e-commerce brands in India.

**Q: Where are you based?**
> We're based in India, primarily operating in IST timezone.

# Product FAQ

**Q: How does Resolvely work?**
> Resolvely connects to your helpdesk and e-commerce platform. When a ticket comes in, our AI reads it, checks relevant data (order status, tracking, return eligibility), and either auto-responds or drafts a response for your team to review. You control how much automation you want.

**Q: What queries can you handle automatically?**
> Our sweet spot is order-related queries:
> - Order status / tracking
> - Return and refund requests
> - Product availability questions
> - Delivery estimates
> - Basic product questions
> 
> Typically, these make up 60-70% of e-commerce support tickets.

**Q: What about complex issues?**
> Complex issues (billing disputes, complaints, technical problems) get routed to your human team with a summary of the context. We don't try to automate everything — just the repetitive stuff.

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
> We currently see 94% accuracy across our customers. When the AI isn't confident, it flags the ticket for human review instead of guessing.

**Q: What platforms do you integrate with?**

| Type | Integrations |
|------|--------------|
| E-commerce | Shopify, WooCommerce, custom (via API) |
| Helpdesk | Freshdesk, Zendesk, Gorgias, Intercom |
| Shipping | Shiprocket, Delhivery, India Post, custom |
| Payments | Razorpay, Cashfree (for refund status) |

**Q: Can you work with our custom platform?**
> Yes, we have an API. If your platform isn't on our standard integrations list, we can usually connect via API. Setup takes a bit longer, and may require some custom work. Let's discuss your specific setup.

**Q: Do you support multiple languages?**
> Currently we support English. Hindi support is coming soon, with Tamil and Telugu planned after.

**Q: Can we customize the AI's responses?**
> Yes. The AI learns from your past tickets and adapts to your tone. You can also set up specific response templates, add your brand's phrases, and exclude certain language.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
> Typical setup is about 5 days for standard integrations. Custom integrations may take a bit longer.

**Q: Do you have an API?**
> Yes. We have a REST API for custom integrations. Documentation is available after signup.

**Q: What about security?**
> - Hosted on AWS (Mumbai region)
> - Data encrypted in transit and at rest
> - We don't store credit card data
> - We can sign an NDA and DPA if required

# Objection Handling

## "I just want to know the pricing"

**Approach:**
> "Totally understand — I want to give you real numbers, not a generic range that might not apply. It's a 15-minute call to understand your volume and setup, then I can give you specific pricing.
>
> What works this week?"

**If they really push:**
> "I hear you. The short version: our pricing is based on ticket volume and starts at a few lakhs annually for smaller brands. But that number means nothing without context — your setup might be more or less.
>
> Seriously, 15 minutes and I'll give you exact numbers. When works?"

---

## "It's too expensive"

*Note: This comes up AFTER a pricing call.*

**Understand the concern:**
> "Help me understand — is it the absolute number, or is it about ROI?"

**Reframe to value:**
> "Let's look at the math: If you're handling [X] tickets and we automate 60%, that's [Y] tickets. Compare that to what another support hire would cost..."

**If still hesitant:**
> "Would a trial help? You can see your actual numbers before committing."

---

## "We're already using [Competitor]"

**Stay curious, not defensive:**
> "Oh nice, how's that working for you? What made you start exploring alternatives?"

**If they mention specific issues:**
> Address those specifically with how we're different.

**If they're just comparing:**
> "Happy to show you how we compare. Usually folks switch for [e-commerce focus / India-specific integrations / how we work]. Want a quick side-by-side?"

---

## "We need to think about it"

**Understand what's unclear:**
> "Totally fair. What's the main thing you're weighing?"

**Offer to help:**
> "Would it help to see how a similar brand is using it? Or talk through any concerns?"

**Set a timeline:**
> "Can I check back [specific day]?"

---

## "Can you just send me more information?"

**Don't just dump materials:**
> "Sure — what specifically would be helpful? I don't want to bury you in docs."

**If they're vague:**
> "Usually folks find a conversation more useful than docs. Can we do 15 minutes this week?"

---

## "Our volume is too low"

**Check the numbers:**
> "What volume are you at? There's a minimum where automation makes sense — let's see if you're there yet."

**If truly low:**
> "Honestly, if you're at [low number], you might not need us yet. Happy to reconnect in a few months when volume grows."

---

## "We want to build this in-house"

**Acknowledge the option:**
> "Totally valid — some teams do. What's your timeline? Building and maintaining this typically takes 3-6 months minimum, plus ongoing work."

**Offer alternative:**
> "What if you use us for 3-6 months while you explore building? You'll have benchmarks either way."

# Sensitive Topics

## If asked about funding / investors:

**Approved response:**
> "We're funded and focused on building. Happy to share more context in person."

**Do NOT share:**
- Specific investor names (unless public)
- Runway or burn rate
- Future fundraising plans

---

## If asked about revenue / growth:

**Approved response:**
> "We're growing steadily — happy to share more specifics when we meet."

**Do NOT share:**
- Specific MRR or ARR
- Churn numbers
- Specific growth percentages

---

## If asked about future product plans:

**Approved response:**
> "We're always building. Hindi support and WhatsApp are near-term. Beyond that, I'd rather not promise specifics — but happy to hear what you'd want to see."

**Do NOT:**
- Promise specific features
- Give specific timelines
- Confirm or deny unannounced features

---

## If asked about competitors:

**Approved response:**
> "We respect [Competitor]. The main differences are [factual points about us]. But honestly, best to try both and see what works for your setup."

**Do NOT:**
- Badmouth competitors
- Make unverifiable claims
- Speculate about their weaknesses

---

## If asked something we can't do:

**Approved response:**

**Always:**
- Be honest about limitations
