## Sales Approach Summary

We use a **network-first, relationship-based** approach:

1. **1st degree contacts** — Personal outreach to people we know
2. **Warm intros** — Referrals from trusted connections
3. **Inbound** — People who reach out to us
4. **No mass cold outreach** — Every conversation should feel personal

**Flow:** Personal connection → Relationship meeting → Demo (if interested) → Pricing call

---

## Pricing Policy

**We don't discuss specific pricing over email.**

When pricing comes up → Route to a short call to understand their needs and give accurate numbers.
